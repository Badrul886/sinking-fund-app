# Product Context

## Always On

The product is a focused sinking-fund planner, not a full budgeting platform.

Core user transformation:
"I have a predictable future expense" → "I know what to save" → "I can see progress" → "I am prepared."

The product should remain:
- simple;
- visual;
- calm;
- trustworthy;
- privacy-conscious;
- mathematically reliable.

Do not expand the MVP into:
- banking;
- investment management;
- tax software;
- full accounting;
- debt management;
- automated bank aggregation.

Bank synchronization requires an explicit product decision and must not be introduced by the agent.
