# Privacy & Sensitive Data

The initial product is privacy-first and manual.

Do not introduce automated bank aggregation.

Do not:
- log sensitive financial values unnecessarily;
- send transaction-level financial data to analytics without explicit justification;
- expose private financial data in error messages;
- hard-code secrets;
- commit credentials.

Local-first/cloud-sync architecture is a product direction, but platform implementation details must be validated before finalization.

Do not make legal claims such as "fully GDPR compliant" without legal review.
